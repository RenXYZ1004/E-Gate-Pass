Public Terms of Service

Terms for use of the SISC e-Gatepass registration service.

1. Purpose of the Service

The e-Gatepass service supports the school's student gate pass registration, identification, exit verification, safety, security, and related communication processes.

2. Accurate Information

Applicants and authorized users are responsible for providing accurate, current, and complete information. Submissions may be reviewed or verified by authorized school personnel.

3. Authorized Use

The service must be used only for legitimate school purposes. Users must not submit another person's information without appropriate authority or attempt to bypass, interfere with, or misuse the school's gate pass and verification procedures.

4. Privacy

Use of this service is subject to the Public Privacy Policy. Information collected through the registration process is used strictly for legitimate school purposes.

5. Verification and Approval

Submitting an application does not by itself guarantee approval or issuance of a gate pass. The school may verify submitted information and apply its current gate pass rules and safety procedures.

6. Service Availability

The school may temporarily suspend, modify, or update the service when necessary for maintenance, security, operational changes, or other legitimate school requirements.

7. Contact

For questions about an application, gate pass requirements, or use of this service, contact the appropriate school administration.
